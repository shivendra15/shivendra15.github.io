About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the BSIM Group, Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS), University of California, Berkeley, USA. I joined UC Berkeley in July 2024 as a Research and Development Engineer, and transitioned to my current Postdoctoral role in December 2024.

My work at the BSIM Group focuses on introducing new features to the BSIM compact models and enhancing the robustness of the existing BSIM models, particularly for BULK and SOI devices. I am also affiliated with the Berkeley Device Modeling Center (BDMC).

I completed my Ph.D. in Microelectronics & VLSI from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, India in 2024, where I worked under the guidance of Prof. Yogesh Singh Chauhan. My doctoral research focused on the Experimental Investigation of FinFET Devices and Circuits at Cryogenic Temperatures and High Frequencies.

My broader research experience spans cryogenic characterization and compact modeling of advanced node transistors (3 nm, 5 nm, 7 nm, 14 nm), RF characterization including harmonic distortion and intermodulation distortion measurements, self-heating modeling, and SRAM analysis for quantum and high-performance computing applications.
